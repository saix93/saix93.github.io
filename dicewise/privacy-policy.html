Dicewise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Developer: Saix

This policy explains what information the mobile game Dicewise ("the game", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: the game itself collects no personal information. It shows ads served by Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to do that, as described below.

Information the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels completed, stars, coins, purchased dice and backgrounds) and your settings (sound, camera view) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city);
  • information about your device and the app, such as device model, operating system version and language;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps, and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

Google uses this information to show ads, which may be personalized if you allow it, to measure their performance, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to prevent fraud. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): when you first open the game, it asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalized ads, which are based on context rather than your profile. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the Privacy options button in the game's main menu.

Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings › Google › All services › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).

Optional reward videos: after a level you can choose to watch a video to double the coins you earned. Watching it is always optional.

Children

Dicewise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and security

Data stored by the game stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own retention policies. Information sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to or restrict its processing. Because the game does not collect personal data itself, most requests are best directed to Google through your Google account. You can also contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, for example by adding analytics or purchases with real money, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
